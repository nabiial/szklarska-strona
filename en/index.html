01 / THE RESIDENCE

Your place in the Karkonosze Mountains. For every season and for a lifetime.

A spacious home in Szklarska Poręba, designed for comfort, privacy and relaxation. Its three levels are clearly divided into leisure, living and sleeping zones.

From the front, the house gives no hint that it conceals an additional level. From the garden side, the lower leisure level opens onto a swimming pool, sauna, games room and a mini bar with a fireplace. Every room on this level has direct access to an open veranda with a small garden, a stone table and a built-in brick barbecue. The ground floor comprises a living room with a fireplace, kitchen and private terrace, a study, a bathroom and a bedroom with its own bathroom. Upstairs are four further bedrooms, each with a private bathroom. Every bathroom in the house is fitted with a toilet and bidet.

The house has underfloor heating, with heating and hot water supplied by a Pereko induction boiler. Security is provided by a CCTV system with a control unit, a Satel Integra alarm system and blinds throughout the house. An automatic gate provides access to the property and garage. Everyday convenience is enhanced by a Beam central vacuum system and Samsung appliances.

This property is suited to a large family as a year-round residence, a holiday home or a rental property, in one of the most beautiful towns in the Karkonosze Mountains, close to hiking trails and ski slopes.
